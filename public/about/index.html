About Mufeed

I am the CEO and Co-founder of Winfunc (YC S24) where we're researching and building products that leverage the emergent properties of AI models, such as reasoning, to augment security audits.

I am and my home is Thrissur, Kerala.

Latest Oscillating Interests: spreadsheets, compilers, and xiaojie cat.

I've done a thing or two

  • Co-founded Winfunc and got into Y Combinator's S24 batch.
  • Created Devika, the open-source alternative to Devin.
  • Represented India at the WorldSkills Olympiad in Cyber Security.
  • Represented India and won the Bronze Medal at the BRICS Skills Olympiad in Cyber Security.
  • Won the Gold Medal at the IndiaSkills Nationals in Cyber Security.
  • Ran a research consultancy company called Lyminal where I solved hard software problems for companies for fun and profit.
  • Patched and reviewed 300+ CVEs in open-source software.
  • Secured Google, Mastercard, Okta, DELL, Ford, Zilliqa, EFF, Telefonica, etc. via Bug Bounty programs.
  • Won the Silver Medal in YIP (Young Innovators Programme) of K-DISC by the Government of Kerala.
  • I loved participating in competitions and have won: FOSSHack 2023, c0c0n CTFs, IndiaSkills, Langjam, etc.
